Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RetailDost

Is my data safe with RetailDost?

Yes. Your data is encrypted locally on your device first, then securely synced to our servers only when you're online. We never share or sell your data.

What if my internet goes down?

RetailDost is designed for offline-first operation. You can continue billing, tracking inventory, and managing your Khata even without internet. Changes sync automatically once you're back online.

Can I use RetailDost on multiple devices?

Yes! Your account can be used on multiple Android phones or tablets. Data syncs seamlessly across all your devices in real-time.

How do I export my data?

You can export sales reports, inventory lists, and Khata details as PDF or CSV files directly from the app. This data belongs to you.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! The Professional plan comes with a 30-day free trial. No credit card required to get started.

What devices does RetailDost support?

RetailDost works on Android 8.0 and above. We're building iOS support for late 2026. Minimum device specs: 2GB RAM, 100MB storage.
